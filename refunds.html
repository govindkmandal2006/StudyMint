Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. General Policy

StudyMint provides digital educational materials. Due to the digital nature of these products, purchases are generally non-refundable once the material has been made available for download.

2. Cancellation

An order cannot normally be cancelled after successful payment and generation of download access.

3. Failed or Unsuccessful Payments

If your payment fails or is unsuccessful, no purchase will be considered complete. If an amount is deducted from your account but the payment is not completed, the amount may be refunded through the payment gateway according to its applicable processing timelines.

4. Duplicate Payment

If you are charged more than once for the same order due to a duplicate transaction, please contact us with the relevant transaction details. We will review the issue and take appropriate action.

5. Technical Issues

If you have successfully paid but cannot access your purchased material because of a technical issue, please contact StudyMint with your order details so that we can investigate and assist you.

6. Contact Us

For refund-related questions or payment issues, please contact us through the Contact Us page.
